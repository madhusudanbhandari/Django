Our Story

Passion on a plate since 2026

A Journey of Flavor

Mero Caafe was born in 2026 with a simple dream — to bring world-class dining to the heart of Kathmandu. What started as a small 20-seat bistro has grown into the city's most celebrated fine dining destination.

Our philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, trust the chef, delight the guest. Every dish on our menu tells a story of tradition, innovation, and love.

We source our produce directly from local farmers in the Kathmandu Valley, ensuring freshness you can taste in every single bite.
